Where each value came from, with its origin line, is DESIGN-artifacts.md at the root. Why this brand and not the eight that were not taken is in the same file. Why dark is the ground, decided against the reading research, is in docs/decisions.md.

2. Palette

Nine values, six of them named roles on the plate and three of them surfaces measured inside the console it drew. Taken as pixels and then checked against the plate's own stylesheet, which agreed at every role. Full sample points in DESIGN-artifacts.md, section 2.

The accent is spent, not applied. The accent carries exactly three things: the tenant's earned latitude in the pane, the live strip, and the selected row. Nothing else in the interface may take it. Count them on the screen above.

A2 was amended at step 5 and the amendment is written down rather than absorbed. Severity now reads as colour rather than as a count of bars, which means saturation is spent in two places instead of one. A2 changed from saturation is scarce to saturation is spent on two closed sets and nothing else. It is still countable: three things on the accent and three levels of severity, and a fourth use of colour anywhere is a defect. concept.md section 5 named this exact conflict before it happened and left the decision to the user, which is where it was made.

4. Geometry

Radius on the interface is zero. Measured on the plate rather than read off a caption: every radius the plate draws is on a physical object, and no chip, button, panel, row or stage box carries one. This overrules the 2px that references.md carried in from Axiom, and it meets attribute A1 more strictly than the reference did.

Depth comes from tone, never from blur. Attribute A4. Three grounds separate the whole console: the board, the panel, and the row being ruled on. There is no shadow and no blur anywhere in the interface, and the only shadows in the whole brand are on the physical objects of the plate.

6. Every attribute, and the element that carries it

An attribute with no element on the screen is a word. This table is what stage 07 checks against, and a row with nothing in its second column is a finding.

AttributeWhat carries it, on the screen above
A1 Precise, not soft--radius-ui: 0 on every interface element, and a 1px rule rather than a shadow at every boundary
A2 Saturation is spent on two closed sets, and on nothing else amendedthe accent carries three things: latitude in the pane, the live strip, the selected row. The severity ramp carries three levels. Both sets are closed and countable, and nothing outside them may take a saturated value

The halation constraint holds by construction. Text on ground is 14.92, not the 21 of pure white on pure black. CLAUDE.md requires that, along with body text at weight 400 or above and open tracking on small monospace, and all three are visible on this page rather than promised.

8. What is deliberately not here

No spacing scale, no semantic token names, no component anatomy, no light theme. Roles only become visible after components have stood on real screens, which is stage 08, and inventing them now means renaming them twice and breaking the origin line on the first rename.

And one thing is not proved yet. This page is one screen. A language is proved on a second, contrasting screen, where the same components stand at a different density and still read as the same product. That is step 7, and until it is done this page is a promising screen rather than a language.

Empty state

Nothing to do, said without apologising. 6 instances across 6 screens: in place of a list 5, and inside a block 1.

An empty queue is not a failure and must not look like one. Nothing waiting is the state the analyst is working towards, and the copy says what is true rather than saying sorry: what the scope currently is, and what would change it.

A slot has no variant matrix. Six molecules turned out to be slots at step 2, measured in a browser: 71 distinct sets of child zones and one computed form each.

When to use it

Where a list would be and there is nothing in it: the queue with no cases waiting, a filter that matched nothing, a block with no rows.

Where she meets it. Rarely, and it is one of the two screens where the product has to be good at not being needed. The other is the outage page.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

Nothing waitingEvery case on your tenants has been ruled on.

The answer first, then the scope that produced it. It tells her the queue is empty and why, so she knows whether to widen the filter.

Do not

No results foundTry adjusting your filters.

Not a stock sentence. It says nothing about which filter, which tenants, or what would change, and an analyst carrying forty tenants cannot act on it.

Limits

Rules of composition and context, which no anti-rule can carry: nothing else is the right component to take, and what is wrong is the count or the neighbour.

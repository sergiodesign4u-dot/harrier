Organism

Document

A screen that is a page rather than a console. 5 instances across 5 screens: the log entry, one column, no pane, no list, read from the top.

It is what node 5.4 is, and node 8.1 turned out to be the same situation under a different name. That is why .z4--solo and .z4--entry were collapsed at stage 07 into one rule: the screen is the page, and a column with no pane beside it does not need two names for the same absence.

It was not in the register. Step 2 built the register from a browser census of controls, and a document body has none of its own. docs/inventory.md section 13 has all thirteen that were missed.

Anatomy

A scaled scene, not a diagram. The zones of a document are its sections, and a block drawing of them would say less than the sections do. This is the real markup in a smaller box: real type, real spacing, real ink. The only fiction is the height, which is 560px here and as long as the record in the product.

the frame around it in the product belongs to frame, not to this

  • .docthe body. A flex column at --space-5 between its sections, padded --space-5 over --space-4. It owns no scroll of its own: Z4 scrolls at the desk and the document scrolls at 360
  • .doc h1--size-xl at weight 600 on --leading-tight. One heading, and it names the record rather than the product
  • h1 .stamplinethe stamp line is part of the heading. A block inside the h1 at --size-sm in mono and --text-secondary, wrapping anywhere. This is the surface an auditor arrives at months later, and the first thing they need is the identifier they can quote
  • @media print16pt of padding, and nothing else. base.css owns the printed surface; this is the document’s share of it, because print is a surface and not a component
  • what fills itblock, claim, prov, addr, anote and tomb. All of them are the case pane’s own parts, and that is the point: the record is the same argument read as a page

Variants

There are none. The empty axes are shown rather than skipped, because two of them were live decisions that closed.

AxisValueUsesThe rule
placementno values–Closed at stage 07 step 6. .z4--entry on node 5.4 and .z4--solo on node 8.1 were one situation under two names, and neither was ever a form of this. The context lives on Z4 and changes how the children paint
widthno values–The measure belongs to frame at --width-frame, 820px. A document that set its own width would fight the frame around it, and the frame is the thing that says what the reader is holding
stateno values–An entry whose snapshot expired, changed or went missing renders the same document with a tomb where the evidence was. The record does not change shape because part of it is gone, which is the whole reason it is a record

When to use it

On a log entry, and on any surface that is read rather than worked. If the analyst is deciding something, the surface is the split and the body is pane body. If somebody is reading what was already decided, it is this.

Where she meets it. Rarely, and never in a hurry. This is the surface of the main job’s last clause, and I can still defend it months later: an auditor, a client asking why, or the analyst herself proving what was known at 04:14 rather than what is known now. It is also the one screen in the product that is designed to be printed.

Never as a stamp under the title. stamp is the atom for the line at the foot of a case pane body, where the analyst has already read the argument. On a document it detaches the name of the record from the record and leaves the heading saying less than it knows.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. There are no verdict controls on this page at any width: you cannot rule on the past, and an Accept here would be a second decision wearing the first one’s date.

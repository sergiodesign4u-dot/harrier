Latitude mark

Two letters wide, and it carries the differentiator. 153 instances across 26 screens, every one of them in the latitude ladder in the case pane and on the fleet. yes 42 times and no 81, and the ratio down that column is the sentence the analyst reads before any of the words beside it.

CLAUDE.md names calibrated trust as the only surviving differentiator, and the fleet as the place it has to read at a glance. This is the component that glance is made of.

The three readings above are the ROW's, not the mark's. The middle row is the ceiling, which takes a surface and weight 600; the last is out of latitude, which takes the whole row to secondary ink. The mark goes with each of them and declares none of them, exactly as key does inside a button.

Variants

None, and it is the second component in this system that reads no colour role at all. key is the other one, for the same reason.

the ceiling–The row's as well. The highest action the agent may take on this tenant gets a surface and weight 600, and the mark inherits both
size–11px, the floor of the scale, and the column is 20px. Both are fixed by the ladder being read as a column rather than as a list of sentences

When to use it

Only in the latitude ladder. It answers one question per row: may Clerk do this, on this tenant, right now. The ladder is the concrete element that carries the strategic dimension, so the component that renders its answer is not reusable decoration.

Where she meets it. Twice: on the case, when deciding whether the agent should have acted rather than asked, and on the fleet, where forty tenants' ladders are the resting state of the detail pane. Design principle 4 says tenant context is never more than a glance away, and this is the shortest possible form of that context.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

yesContain a single endpoint

Two letters, then the action, on one line, in a ladder where every row starts at the same place. What is scanned is the column, not the sentences.

Do not

yes

Not as a badge. A boundary around it would make it a thing that reports an outcome, which is state, and would break the column: the ladder is read down the left edge, and 40 boxes read as 40 objects rather than one answer repeated.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. It is a reading of the record and there is nothing to press.

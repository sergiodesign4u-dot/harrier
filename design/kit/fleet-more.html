Fleet more

It says what is not on the screen. 10 instances across 10 screens, at the foot of the fleet.

The pane holds a handful of tenants and the analyst carries 40 or more, so the last line of the fleet names how many are below the fold. It is the count that stops the visible rows from reading as the whole fleet, and it is the difference between a summary and a sample.

When to use it

At the foot of the fleet, when there are more tenants than the pane shows. Ten of the sixteen fleet renderings carry it, and the other six show every tenant they have.

Where she meets it. While glancing at the fleet, which is most of a shift. The line is what tells her the glance is a sample and how big a sample it is.

Rule and anti-rule

Do not

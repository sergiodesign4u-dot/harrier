Atom

Hint

One line saying what the interface will not do for you. 18 instances across 18 screens: under a field 7, and in a dialog footer 11.

Design principle 2 says the cheapest correct thing first, and a hint is the cheapest correct thing there is: one line, secondary ink, and it never repeats the label above it. A reason is required, One selection, one key, No password here. Your email decides which provider signs you in.

When to use it

When the analyst would otherwise have to try something to find out. It is not help text and it is not a description: it names a constraint or removes a doubt, in one line, and if it needs two lines it is the wrong component.

Where she meets it. In the reject dialog, mostly, which is the one place in this product where she is asked for something rather than shown something. Reject costs four taps against accept's two, and the hint is what stops the fourth from being a surprise.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

A reason is required

The constraint, in the analyst's words, before she runs into it. Four words, secondary ink, and it says nothing the label already said.

Do not

Node 4.4 section 2. The reason goes straight into tuning.

Not for an argument about the design. That is anote, which has its own register and its own rail. A node number in a hint is a count of the product's own parts standing in the interface, which voice.md forbids.

Limits

Rules of composition and context, which no anti-rule can carry: nothing else is the right component to take, and what is wrong is the count or the neighbour. Counted on the grey corpus at stage 09, and every one of them is a function in design/kit/checks/rules.mjs. Full table with sources on Architecture.

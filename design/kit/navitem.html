Navigation item

The global navigation, and the whole of it: three items in the MVP. 132 instances across 44 screens, and not one of them is in an HTML file. design/_shell.js writes the bar, which is why this component stood on 132 nodes for four stages without a class or a name.

Clients arrives with cluster 7 and is not here. The fleet has no item of its own and never will: it is the resting state of the Queue's right pane, so it costs zero taps and would cost one if it were a fourth item.

When to use it

Only in Z1, and only for the three clusters of intent. This is the one component in the system whose count is settled by the IA rather than by a screen: ia/docs/sitemap.md decides what is in the bar, and a fourth item arrives when a cluster does, not when a screen needs a shortcut.

Where she meets it. At the start of a shift and almost never afterwards. Rasha lives in the Queue for six hours, and the bar exists so that the two other places she has to answer for, the Shift and the Log, are one click away rather than a search. It is the least used control in the product and it still has to say where she is at a glance, because a case opened from a pager at 03:00 lands her somewhere she did not choose.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

QueueShift

One current item, ink and underline together. The bar answers where am I before it answers where can I go.

Do not

Open this entry in the log

Not for moving between screens inside the page. That is link. A navitem is a place in the product's structure; a link is a way to something particular, and the difference is exactly the one the underline makes.

And not for the fleet. The fleet is the resting state of the Queue's right pane, decided in CLAUDE.md and not reopened here: giving it a navitem would put a tap between the analyst and the thing this product is differentiated on.

Limits

Rules of composition and context, which no anti-rule can carry: nothing else is the right component to take, and what is wrong is the count or the neighbour. Counted on the grey corpus at stage 09, and every one of them is a function in design/kit/checks/rules.mjs. Full table with sources on Architecture.

  • R10. The fleet has no route and no navigation item. A route would make the differentiator a place you go.
  • R5. No shell before authentication, and no annunciator on a console that cannot read Clerk.

This component is why --text-hover exists. It has neither a ground nor a boundary it could move: a plate under a nav item makes it read as a button, and a boundary competes with the underline that means you are here. Ink is the only surface left, and the surface rule forbids borrowing --line-hover for it even though the two are the same colour today. The three together say one sentence: hover raises whatever it touches to full strength on its own surface.

Active is the same move as hover, declared rather than left out so nobody adds a third step later thinking it was forgotten: the navigation follows immediately and there is nothing a pressed state could say that the arrival does not. Out of reach does not exist, and the rule that drew it is the row above.

Motion

Response, 120ms, the browser's own ease. What moves is ink, the whole global navigation, three items. Named one by one rather than as all, because all animates what nobody ordered and drags the layout properties in behind it. Never a size and never a position: those make the browser recalculate the layout of the page on every frame. Under prefers-reduced-motion it is 1ms, and this component does nothing to make that happen: it reads var(--dur-fast) and the token is redefined once. Full reasoning on Motion.

What it reads, and where it stands

Ink and one line, no fill at all. --text-secondary, --text-primary, --text-hover, --line-current. Geometry straight from primitive: --size-md and --space-2.

Copy this

<nav aria-label="Sections"> <a class="navitem is-current" href="queue.html" aria-current="page">Queue</a> <a class="navitem" href="shift.html">Shift</a> <a class="navitem" href="entry.html">Log</a> </nav>

You will not write this by hand. design/_shell.js emits the whole bar from one call, and step 6 is what puts the class into the generator. aria-current="page" and .is-current carry the same fact to two audiences and both are required.

Atom

Stamp

One line at the foot of the case body, 24 instances across 24 screens, and it is the part of the interface that has to still be true months later. Who filed it, when, and the address of the evidence as it stood.

The main job in CLAUDE.md ends with and I can still defend it months later. Geo and compliance require an append-only log carrying the evidence snapshot at decision time. The stamp is where that requirement becomes a string on a screen.

When to use it

Once per case pane, at the foot of the body, and nowhere else. It is not a general timestamp: it is the provenance of one filed opinion. A time that appears in a row or a log entry is that surface's own column and not this component.

Where she meets it. Twice, months apart. Once while deciding, where it tells her how fresh Clerk's work is; and once when somebody asks why she ruled the way she did, where the ?as-of address is the whole answer. The second reading is the one this component exists for, and it is the one nobody is in the room for.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

The actor, the elapsed time and the address, in that order, at the foot. Quiet, complete, and quotable.

Do not

Filed 26m ago

Not without the address. An elapsed time alone dates the opinion and does not reproduce the evidence, and reproducing the evidence is the requirement. The relative time also stops being true the moment the page is printed, which is a surface this product really has.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. It is the record, and a record you can point at is a record somebody expects to change.

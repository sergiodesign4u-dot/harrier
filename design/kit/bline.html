Brief line

One counted fact from the shift you are picking up. 19 instances across 7 screens, in the counted blocks of the shift brief, and it is a line and not a card.

The brief’s own block table ruled out the alternative: four counted facts as four cards is a dashboard, and a handover is read once from the top. Three zones in one order: the number, what it counts, and where it goes.

The number is the one place in the product at --size-lg in mono at 700. 17px is the largest a figure gets anywhere, and weight 700 is licensed on a counted value only. The brief is read standing up at the start of a shift, so the count is what carries across the desk and the sentence is what you lean in for.

It was not in the register. Step 2 built the register from a browser census of controls, and this is a text composition with no control in it. Found at step 5 by scanning every class that owns a rule block in kit.css against the register: twenty one came back and thirteen were components. docs/inventory.md section 13 has all of them.

  • .nthe count, right aligned in its fixed 52px column. That column is why 18 and 6 line up on the unit down a block instead of drifting with the width of the figure

When to use it

In a counted block of the shift brief, once per fact the incoming analyst has to hold: what waits on a decision, what Clerk closed alone, what is still being gathered. Four of them stack into a block and the block is read down in one pass. Anywhere the fact is not a count, this is the wrong component.

Where she meets it. Twice a day at most, at the two moments the shift changes hands, and each time under time pressure from somebody waiting to leave. That is the whole reason the figure is the largest thing on the surface: the second of the three MVP jobs is pick up and hand off a shift, and the brief has to be readable before she has sat down.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

18waiting on a decision across 12 of 40 tenants in your scopeopen the queue →

A figure, what the figure counts with its scope, and where to go and see it. The count names its claim and its scope, and the destination is on the same line as the number that earned it.

Do not

somecases were closed while you were awaynowhere

Never a word in the number column. 17px at weight 700 is licensed on a counted value and on nothing else, and a word set there claims a precision it does not have. An uncounted fact under a block is gnote, which is the quiet line built for exactly that.

At 360 the three columns hold: 52px for the figure, the rest for the sentence, and .go pinned right at its own width. The sentence wraps because its column is minmax(0,1fr) rather than 1fr, which is the difference between a grid that wraps and a grid that pushes the destination off the screen.

States

Only the link form has any. Point at the first line below and it takes a ground; press and it takes a second one. Point at the one under it and nothing happens, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Out of reach does not exist here, and that is a decision rather than an omission. Three of the nineteen lead somewhere and sixteen do not, and the sixteen are written without the a. A disabled link would say the destination exists and is closed to you; what is true is that there is nothing to open, and nowhere to review in the third column says so in words.

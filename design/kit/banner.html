When to use it

At the top of a zone, when something about the whole zone has changed: Clerk is down, the connection is reconnecting, this case has already been acted on. 20 of them sit in Z4 above the queue and 16 in the detail pane.

Where she meets it. On arrival, and it is the component that has to earn its interruption. Fifty four instances across forty four screens is close to the ceiling: one more reason to show a banner and the analyst starts reading past them.

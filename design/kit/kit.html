Stage 07 · the kit

Component kit

Everything the product is assembled from, drawn by kit.css and by nothing else. A screen is not painted, it is built out of these classes. A detail that is missing goes into the kit first and onto a screen second, and a fix made on one screen is a desync rather than a fix.

The census that decided what is here, and the level of each thing, is inventory.md. What the extract wanted and was not allowed to decide is proposed-variables.md.

rules, and which one a component takes is a WCAG decision

--rule-hair separates rows and carries no meaning, so it sits below every floor deliberately. --rule-edge draws panel edges at 2.997, which is under 3:1 and allowed because 1.4.11 covers controls, not dividers. --rule-control is the boundary that identifies a control and clears 3:1.

.bars · severity, 225 uses, 34 pages

A count of lit bars and a colour. The ramp descends in chroma rather than in brightness, because on a dark ground every value has to clear 4.5:1 and loudness cannot carry a ramp. Warm to cool and never to green: a low severity case is not a resolved one.

High Medium Low

The colour is selected on the drawing, not on a class: the markup carries three bars and some are lit, and :has() reads the count. That is what let the whole ramp land on 32 screens without adding one attribute to any of them.

form primitives · in the kit at a single occurrence

A form control enters at one use rather than two: it is a primitive of interaction and a system without it is incomplete. Checkbox, radio and toggle do not exist in this product at all, and that is an open decision in proposed-variables.md.

icons · defined, and applied to nothing

The product contains zero SVG across all 62 screens. Two thirds of the content axis in inventory.md is therefore empty, and every icon added is a decision with no wireframe behind it. The set exists here so it can be judged; it is not on a single screen. 24px grid, 1.5px stroke, square terminals to match a radius of zero, and currentColor, so an icon inherits the severity or the accent of whatever it sits in.

the shell · .wf-shell, .wf-screen, .z1, .z2, .z45, .z4, .z5, .z6

Z1 and Z2 appear in zero html files. They are injected at runtime, which is why an inventory taken from the screens alone has no header in it and does not notice. The markup they produce is written once in shell.html, and that is the file a screen copy takes.

The pane at rest holds the fleet, and that is the decision that gives per-tenant latitude zero taps. Its empty state must read as this is the fleet, never as this is empty.

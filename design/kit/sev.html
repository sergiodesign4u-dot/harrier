Molecule

Severity

Two things in a fixed arrangement, and the arrangement is the whole point: the glyph is the glance and the word is the answer. 225 instances across 34 screens.

It is the smallest molecule in the system and it earns the level. Neither part is optional and the order never changes. Take the word away and it is a graphic carrying information available nowhere else; take the bars away and it is a word in a column of two hundred.

When to use it

In the first cell of a queue row, and nowhere else today. It is the first thing read and the thing that sorts eighteen rows into an order.

Where she meets it. Scanning, before she has read a single client name. That is why it is a shape AND a colour AND a word: the shape carries at a metre, the colour carries at a glance, and the word carries when neither does.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

Medium

Always both parts, always in this order. The word is also what makes the unlit slot legal below 3:1.

Do not

Never the bars alone. That is bars, which is an atom and not a severity: on its own it is a graphic the analyst has to learn before she can read the queue, and a WCAG 1.4.11 failure with it.

This molecule is why bars.css is allowed to draw its unlit slot at 2.997. 1.4.11 exempts a graphic whose information is available in text, and the text is here, on every one of the 225. The line is written in both files: drop the word from this one and the other stops being correct.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. The row around it has a hover and a selected ground; severity does not change because you pointed at it.

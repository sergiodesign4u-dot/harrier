Foundations

Typography

Two families, five sizes, three weights and two line heights, and every one of them is stage 04's, inherited rather than re-derived. Small and short on purpose: design principle 5 says density is the feature, and six hours a day means no decorative whitespace.

Two families

Chosen on a rendered comparison of four candidates each rather than on the name drawn on the brandbook plate. Archivo carries display and body; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for anything counted, which is what makes a number legible as a number at a glance rather than as a word.

Neither family is loaded, and that is a finding rather than a decision. Nothing in the product links a webfont: no @font-face, no stylesheet from a font host, on any of the 52 coloured pages. Both names resolve from the reader's own machine. On a machine that has them the product looks as designed; everywhere else it falls back to system-ui and ui-monospace, and the character both families were chosen for is gone. The scale

The scale is in rem since stage 10, and two of its five sizes grow with the window. It was in px from stage 04 to stage 09: at a 16px root those are the same numbers, and for a reader who set a larger font a px scale simply ignores the setting, which is how a layout fails WCAG 1.4.4 quietly. The numbers below are what they resolve to at this window's width, measured, and the two fluid ones move if you drag the edge.

measuring

The two ends of each clamp are this product's own two widths, 1280 and 1920, so the type grows across exactly the band the analyst sits in and is stage 04's value everywhere below it. The three small sizes do not grow at all: density is the feature, and on a wider monitor this analyst wants more rows rather than larger ones. What makes dense text readable is the measure, and that is capped at 66ch. A font-size is never switched at a point, and the middle term of each clamp carries a rem addend rather than pure vw, because pure vw takes the page out of the reader's zoom. HARRIER

The product's own tracking already meets what the dark ground needs, which is why forcing the token onto it would be the wrong way round. CLAUDE.md requires slightly open tracking on small monospace because of halation, and stage 04's spread of .04 to .14em is open throughout. Making the six into one to make a token look used would change stage 04's typography for the sake of the token. The question this page asks is the other one: should six values be six, and if not, which one survives.

Atom

Input

The one component whose whole job is to be typed into. 34 instances across 15 screens, always inside a field: the rejection reason, the escalation recipient, the client summary draft, the sign in form and the queue's search.

Three rows of the step 2 register turned out to be one component, and writing the file is what found it. input, textarea and select were listed separately. The splitting criterion is anatomy, meaning the set of child zones and their order, and all three have the same anatomy: none at all. What differs is which element the browser needs for the kind of answer being collected, and that is a variant axis. The register itself already treated it as one a level up, where field carries control: textarea 21, input 9. Atoms go from 19 to 17 and the register from 62 to 60.

Variants

One axis, three values, and the axis declares nothing at all. Look at the file: below the comment there is no rule. The three take the same single declaration and differ only in the element.

controlUsesRenderedThe rule
input9 one line. An answer that is a name, an address or a search
textarea21 several lines, and how many is rows in the markup rather than CSS: 2 for a note, 3 for a rejection reason, 4 for an amendment. How much room to offer is a fact about the question being asked, not about the component
select4 the native control, never a drawn one. A drawn select has to reimplement the popup, the keyboard and the screen reader, and gets one of the three right. It is also the one value with no coloured rendering anywhere: all four stand on the keyboard map, which keeps its eleven one-off classes and is outside the system until step 6 rules otherwise
size–prohibited one size. A field smaller than the body text is a field somebody will mistype into
error0a row in the backlog The product has no error state on a field, and it does have a case that needs one: the reject dialog holds its main action out of reach until a reason is given, and says so in a hint rather than on the field. That is a defensible answer and it is not a drawn one. Named here rather than invented, and it goes to backlog.md

When to use it

Wherever the product has to collect something it cannot know. That is a short list on purpose: an adjudication console asks the analyst for almost nothing, because everything it needs it has already correlated. 21 of the 34 are one question, why you are rejecting, and design principle 3 is the reason: the reason goes straight into tuning, so the field is the part of the interface that teaches.

Where she meets it. Only when she disagrees with Clerk. Accept is two taps and touches no field at all; reject costs four and the field is the third of them. That asymmetry is measured and named in CLAUDE.md, and this component is where the extra cost is actually paid.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

Why you are rejecting

Always inside a field, always with a label above it. The label is a separate component and it is not optional: a box with no name is a box people guess at.

Do not

Not with the label inside it as a placeholder. It disappears the moment she starts typing, which is exactly when she needs to check what was asked, and it is the one field in this product she will be quoted on months later.

Live, in both themes. Point at the field, then reach it with Tab and watch the ring arrive outside a boundary that does not go away.

The boundary moved, and it is the one appearance change in this file.

kit.css drew it with --color-edge at 2.997 against the page. A text field is identified by nothing but its boundary: the label sits outside it and an empty field has no content to read. That is precisely what WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of, and 2.997 is a number that rounds to the threshold and does not meet it, which is a mistake this project has now made three times. It reads --rule-control at 3.98, the role that exists for exactly this, and the row is in tokens-audit.md. 34 fields change, and it is the only difference the pixel comparison at step 8 will find in this component.

What it reads, and where it stands

One fill, one ink, two lines: --bg-page, --text-primary, --rule-control, --line-hover, and --color-focus through base.css. Geometry straight from primitive: --size-md, --font-sans, --leading, --radius-ui, --space-2 and --space-3.

Copy this

<textarea class="input" id="why" rows="3"></textarea> <input class="input" id="email" type="email" autocomplete="username"> <select class="input" id="scope"><option>...</option></select>

The class goes on the element and never on a wrapper, so the three stay one component. Step 6 replaces .field input, .field textarea, .field select with it, which is a rename map row.

Molecule

Claim

The line of evidence. 131 instances across 24 screens, and after the row it is the most repeated thing in the product.

Three zones in one order: what kind of claim this is, the claim, and where it came from. Design principle 2 is built out of these: the tag lets her skip, the sentence lets her judge, and the source lets her check.

It was not in the register. Step 2 built the register from a browser census of controls, and this is a container. Found at step 5 by scanning every class that owns a rule block in kit.css against the register: twenty one came back and thirteen were components. docs/inventory.md section 13 has all of them.

Behaviour at width

Below 1280, the system's one point (Width), the line wraps and the source takes margin-left:auto. That is the whole of it. Until then the source parked at the top right and the sentence wrapped underneath it, so a two line claim read as two columns; the source takes a line of its own now. The three zones, their order, and the tag sitting inside .txt rather than beside it are the same at every width.

When to use it

In the evidence block of a case, once per thing Clerk found or looked for. It is the unit the analyst reads down until she either believes the verdict or does not.

Where she meets it. Dozens of times a shift, on the one surface the whole product exists for. Six hours a day is why the tag exists at all: it is what lets her read the fourth claim without reading the third.

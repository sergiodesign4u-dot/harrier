Molecule

Head states

The case’s condition, next to the case’s name. 7 instances across 7 screens, under the title in a pane head.

The states are the same component the queue row carries in its states cell. What this molecule does is put them where the analyst is already looking when she opens a case, so that escalated or unrecorded is read before the evidence rather than after the decision.

It is one declaration and it still earns a name. A row of states under a pane title is a composition that recurs, and the alternative is seven pane heads each declaring the same wrapping flex. It is the smallest molecule in the system after sev, and both are here for the same reason: the arrangement is the component.

When to use it

Under a pane title, when the case is in a condition worth knowing before reading it. Not every case has one: 7 of the 48 pane heads carry this.

Where she meets it. The instant a case opens. CLAUDE.md binds a rule on this layer: a case that left the analyst’s hands must not look identical to one that did not. In the queue that rule is the states cell; in the pane it is this.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

decidedunrecorded

The conditions the case is in, under its name, before anything else. Two facts and no sentence.

Do not

This case has been decided but the verdict was not recorded.

Not as prose. The condition is a closed set of words the analyst reads in the queue all day, and turning it into a sentence in the pane makes her read the same fact twice in two forms.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. The states inside it have none either, which makes this the only molecule in the system where nothing at any level can be pressed.

Molecule

Field

Three atoms in one order, and the order is the component. 30 instances across 15 screens.

Label, control, hint. The label is never inside the control, the hint is never above the label, and the gap between them is one step of the scale so the three read as one thing rather than three.

It is the clearest molecule in the system. Three atoms, a direction and a gap, and everything a reader expects to find here is somewhere else on purpose: the box is input, the name is label, the constraint is hint, and each of them stands alone on other screens.

When to use it

Wherever the product has to collect something it cannot know. That is a short list on purpose: an adjudication console asks the analyst for almost nothing, because everything it needs it has already correlated.

Where she meets it. Only when she disagrees with Clerk. 21 of the 30 are one question, why you are rejecting, and design principle 3 is the reason: the reason goes straight into tuning, so the field is the part of the interface that teaches.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

Label above, control below, one step apart. Clicking the label focuses the control, which the browser does for free once the elements are right.

Do not

Why you are rejecting

Not with the name in the placeholder and not with the hint repeating it. The placeholder disappears when she starts typing, and a hint that repeats the label spends a line saying nothing.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive.

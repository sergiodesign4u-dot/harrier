Molecule

Rota

Who is on call, read from the provider’s own tool. 7 instances across 7 screens, in the shift brief.

Harrier reads this and never owns it, so there is no control anywhere in the component: only the name node 4.6 needs when an escalation has to go to somebody. That dependency is why the open question about who the secondary persona is blocks a mechanism rather than a paragraph.

When to use it

In the shift brief, once, next to what moved. It exists so that an escalation has a recipient and the analyst does not have to leave the product to find out who that is.

Where she meets it. At the start of a shift, and again at 03:00 when something has to go to a human. It is read twice and never touched.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

S. VargaSOC leadon now

A name, a role and a window, with the one that matters given a ground. Enough to address an escalation and nothing more.

Do not

Change the rota

Never a control. The rota belongs to the provider’s on call tool, and a button here would either lie or write into a system this product does not own.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive. Point at any line and nothing happens, which is the component working.

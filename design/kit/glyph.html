Atom

Glyph

A drawing standing inside a control. 140 instances across 46 screens, and every one of them replaced a character that had been typed into product copy on 2026-08-26.

Anatomy

One box, one mask, no colour of its own. It is sized in em so it follows whatever it stands in: a chip is 11px and a digest line is 12.5, and a glyph carrying a pixel size would be the wrong size in one of them.

All tenants Waiting on a decision open the queue
<i class="glyph glyph--menu" aria-hidden="true"></i>

It is always aria-hidden. There is no case in this product where the drawing is the only thing saying what a control does: the chip carries its filter's name, the toast's dismiss carries an aria-label, and the digest's line is a sentence. A glyph that has to be announced is a glyph in the wrong place.

Variants

--menu81there is more under this. The expand glyph, the same drawing the disclosure and the select carry, and it means the same thing in all three
--dismiss56this goes away. The reject glyph, one rung down from the verdict: reject a case, dismiss a filter, dismiss a notice
--next3the seventeenth glyph and the only new drawing here. The digest's routes ended in a typed arrow and the set had nothing to replace it with. Same 24 grid, same 1.5 weight, and it is escalate turned a quarter

Why it exists

An icon typed as a character is still an icon, and it is the worst kind. Three were standing in this product as text, and each one cost four things at once.

A different drawing everywherethe shape, the weight and the optical size of U+25BE come from whichever font the platform resolves. Two of the three were already in icons.js as real geometry and the product was ignoring them
It is read alouda screen reader says times and black down-pointing small triangle. The cross on a toast was spared that by an aria-label; the 137 on chips sat inside the button's accessible name
It is not aligneda text glyph sits on the baseline at whatever height its font gives it. chip.css records, in as many words, that one scope bar held three chips at three heights and that the caret glyph is taller than the cross
Nothing could find itevery check here reads roles, tokens, contrast or geometry, and a character in a string is none of those. design/kit/checks/glyphs.mjs reads every character the product renders that is not a letter, a digit or ordinary punctuation

What is not a glyph

The line is the character's job, not its code point. The same census found 365 more and none of them moves here.

·235a separator, which is punctuation. It has an ink role of its own, --text-divider, and the contrast sweep exempts it with that reason written down
–30a reading of no value in a record cell. Punctuation
↑ ↓100the name of the key somebody presses, inside a kbd or a key. Drawing those would say the key has a picture on it, and it has a character on it, the same as a and Enter

States

None, and it is never the control. It stands inside one. The chip, the toast's dismiss and the digest's line carry hover, focus and out of reach, and the glyph takes their ink through currentColor without declaring anything. A glyph that lit up on its own would be a second control inside the first.

What it reads, and where it stands

Colournone. currentColor only, which makes it the second atom in the system that takes no colour role, after key and for the same reason
Geometry1em square, vertical-align: -0.09em, and the 24 unit drawing scaled by contain
The second copya CSS mask cannot read JavaScript, so each drawing is carried twice: once in design/system/icons.js and once as a data URI here. design/kit/checks/icons.mjs decodes every mask it finds and compares it against the set character for character, which is the only thing that keeps the copy honest

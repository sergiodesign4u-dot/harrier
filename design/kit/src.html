Atom

Source

The end of a line of evidence: where it came from. 131 instances across 24 screens, and the second most common thing in the case pane after the claim itself.

It carries the only icon this product really needed. Design principle 5 puts density first and the product read perfectly with none, so each of the three applied icons had to be argued. This is the strongest of the three: an evidence line names its source, and nothing said the source opens somewhere else. It is attached as a CSS mask rather than markup, because wireframes/ is frozen and every coloured page holds a structural diff of exactly zero against its grey original.

When to use it

At the end of a claim, naming the system the evidence came out of: Entra sign in, Exchange audit, Entra, EDR, tenant baseline. Design principle 2 says Clerk shows its work up to a ceiling, and an evidence trail alone is table stakes: what earns trust is that the claim names its source and the source is reachable.

Where she meets it. On the one surface the whole product exists for, dozens of times a shift. She reads the claim; if she believes it she moves on, and if she does not she opens the source. That second path is the reason this component is a link at all, and it is why the 13 that are not links matter enough to have been found.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

Entra sign in

Name the system, not the URL, and let the icon say it opens away from here. Two or three words, mono, at the end of the line.

Do not

Entra sign in

Not as a plain link. A link stays inside the product and says nothing about leaving it; the icon is the whole difference, and without it the analyst finds out where she is going by arriving there.

States

Live, in both themes. Point at the source in the first claim, then at the one in the second and watch nothing happen, because the second one is not a link.

The word keeps the claim's ink in every state, and here that matters more than anywhere. 18 of the sources sit inside claim--absence, where Clerk found nothing and the whole line is dimmed. A source that brightened under the pointer would say the absence had stopped being one.

Active and out of reach are absent for the same reasons as on link, and the span form is the product's own answer to the second one: where the evidence cannot be reached, the source is written without a link at all rather than drawn as a disabled one.

What it reads, and where it stands

No colour role of its own. It inherits the ink of the claim, and the icon is painted with currentColor, so it dims when the claim does. Geometry: --font-mono and --size-xs.

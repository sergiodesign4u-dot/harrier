Organism

Pane body

The surface the whole product exists for. Everything between the pane’s head and its foot: the verdict, the evidence, the latitude ladder and the stamp. 38 instances across 38 screens.

It is a slot, and that is measured rather than assumed. 38 instances show 13 distinct sets of child zones and one computed form. There is nothing to switch here: what changes is what somebody put in it, and everything in it is another component.

Five steps of gap between blocks and two inside them. That ratio is the whole of the reading rhythm on the densest surface in the product: sections are far apart so the eye can find one, and lines inside a section are close so it can read down. Design principle 5 says density is the feature, and this is where the density is spent rather than saved.

Anatomy

What the bench is standing in. A 340px box at --pane-w carrying the pane’s raised ground and its overflow-y, which is z5. The body itself declares no height and no scroll: it is a column that grows, and the pane around it is what runs out of room. Scroll inside the bench and the head stays while the evidence goes past.

Behaviour at width

Below 1280, the system's one point (Width), the padding drops from --space-4 to --space-3. That is the only rule in the file, and the five steps of gap between blocks and two inside them are untouched: the reading rhythm is what this surface is for, and a narrower window is not a reason to change it. Above the point the body is as wide as the pane, which grows from 320px at 1280 to 544 at 2560.

Once inside every detail pane that is showing a case, between pane-head and pane-foot. Not for the fleet: the pane’s resting state has no zone in common with a case past the head, and making it one would have written the fleet as a case with nothing in it.

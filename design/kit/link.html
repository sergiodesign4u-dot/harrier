Atom

Link

24 instances across 24 screens, inside whatever encloses them. It takes the ink of its host and colours nothing but its own underline, which is what lets the same element sit in a grey note, in an expansion and in a severity line without becoming a fourth thing in each.

It had no declaration anywhere, and that is what made it dangerous rather than merely unnamed. Stage 07 shipped 151 browser blue links across 27 screens, in the one stage whose contract says there is no colour. Nothing declared them, so nothing flagged them: the defect was an ABSENT rule, and an instrument that greps can only see rules that exist. It took asking the browser for a computed colour to find it.

  • text-decoration-colorthe only thing it paints. --text-secondary at rest, so the underline is visible without competing with the sentence it is in
  • text-underline-offset2px. The type is 12px in most of its hosts and an underline on the baseline eats the descenders

Two rules for the same thing, and both are needed. base.css keeps a declaration on the bare a as the safety net: a link that carries no class still cannot come back blue. This file is the component, and step 6 puts the class on the markup.

When to use it

Inside running text, for something particular. The 24 instances are almost all the same job: a note explaining what Clerk did, naming the thing it read, and offering it. That is gnote, 15 of the 24.

Where she meets it. Reading the evidence, deciding whether to go and check. Design principle 2 says the cheapest correct thing first and depth one key away: the link is the depth. It has to be visible enough to be found on the second read and quiet enough not to be found on the first, which is the whole reason the underline carries it and the word does not.

Do not

Not as a control standing on its own. Something that stands alone and is pressed is btn, and it claims a boundary and a place in the zone's one main action. A link that has been given a border is a button that forgot to say so.

And not for a source. A line of evidence naming where it came from is src, 131 instances, and it carries the external icon because the source opens outside the case. Using a link there loses the one signal that says you are leaving.

The word keeps its host's colour in every state. Changing it on hover would make a link inside a severity line change severity for as long as the pointer is on it, which is the one thing this component was named to stop.

Two states are absent and each says why. Active: a link navigates on release, so the pressed moment and the page change are the same event and nothing would be seen. A button is different, and that is exactly why it has one: its press may not complete. Out of reach: a link that cannot be followed is not a link. Where the product has something to name and nowhere to send you it writes the words without a link at all, which is what gnote does on the four screens where the evidence has fallen out of the retention window.

Motion

Response, 120ms, the browser's own ease. What moves is an underline, and only its own underline: it takes the ink of whatever encloses it. Named one by one rather than as all, because all animates what nobody ordered and drags the layout properties in behind it. Never a size and never a position: those make the browser recalculate the layout of the page on every frame. Under prefers-reduced-motion it is 1ms, and this component does nothing to make that happen: it reads var(--dur-fast) and the token is redefined once. Full reasoning on Motion.

What it reads, and where it stands

Two roles, both on the underline, and none on the word: --text-secondary and --text-hover. Geometry: nothing but the 2px offset, because the size belongs to the container.

Atom

Trend

Which way the record is going. 72 instances across 16 screens, always inside rec and never anywhere else. Without it the count is not evidence.

31 of 36 says what the agent's record is; was 34 of 36 says which way it is moving, and the direction is the whole of what CLAUDE.md calls calibrated trust: that the trust is earned and visible rather than asserted. A record with no trend is a number the analyst has to remember last week's value of, forty times over, which is precisely the failure mode the riskiest assumption in this project names.

When to use it

Inside a record, always, and nowhere else. The pairing is the component: a count without a trend and a trend without a count are each half of an argument, and the fleet exists to make the whole argument readable at a glance.

Where she meets it. When deciding whether to give the agent more rope on a tenant, which is the third of the three MVP jobs and the one CLAUDE.md calls a bet rather than a qualified job. If that bet is wrong, this is the component that was carrying it.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

4 of 4too few to compare

Say there is no trend when there is none. A tenant with four decisions is the case this component is most likely to be wrong about, and the honest string is shorter than the dishonest one.

Do not

4 of 4was 3 of 3

Not a trend built from three decisions. It is true, it is useless, and it reads exactly like a trend built from three hundred, which is how a number in this interface stops being evidence.

States

It has none, and it is not interactive.

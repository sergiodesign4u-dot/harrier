Molecule

Annunciator

The one fixed slot that reports on Clerk. Three parts in one order: what the agent is doing, what it has done, and who is answering for it. Node 0.3, read only in the MVP.

55 renderings and zero html files. design/_shell.js writes it, exactly like navitem, z1 and z2. An inventory taken from the screens alone cannot see it, which is precisely why it was missing from the register: it has no class in any file. Of the 55, 31 screens pass a tenant reading of their own and the rest take the fleet reading, which the shell holds as its default.

It changes on the tenant, never on the arrow key. With nothing selected it reads the fleet; with a case selected it reads that tenant. The accessible name follows the state for the same reason, because one fixed name would be false in one of the two readings.

The count is 700 and that is allowed. Attribute A3 permits weight 700 on a counted value only, and this is one of the two places in the product that qualifies. The other is readout, which was breaking the same rule until round 2 measured it.

Anatomy

Both halves below scroll sideways, and the reason is this page rather than the component. The strip in its nowrap form measures 700px for the reading shown, and a half of this article at 1440 gives it 525px. In z1 it has the width of the top bar left over after the wordmark, three navigation items, the keyboard map and the analyst’s name, which is where the 1280 defect below was found. When to use it

Nowhere by hand. It is a fixed slot in the top bar, written by the shell on every signed in screen, and it is placed by z1 rather than by a page. Signed out, z1 drops it along with the navigation and the keyboard map, because there is no tenant and no analyst to report on.

Where she meets it. Continuously, and mostly without looking at it. It is above every screen for six hours a day, which is the argument for 11px mono and no ornament: it has to be legible when she looks and silent when she does not. The one moment it is read deliberately is when she is deciding whether Clerk should have acted rather than asked, and the second part of the strip is the answer.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

FLEET|40 tenants|1 moved down

The scope, then the count, then what changed. Every accented word is a figure, and the strip says what is true about the machine including the part that got worse.

Do not

FLEET|40 TENANTS|all healthy

Never the inverted plate on anything but an override. A case condition that is merely worth noticing is state, which carries thirteen words on three surfaces and is built for exactly that. Inverting a count spends the strip’s loudest register on a number that is true every day.

It overflowed at 1280, which is the product’s declared minimum, so this was a defect at the width the spec names. Nowrap plus a third part pushed the analyst’s name past the right edge, measured at 1344 in a 1280 window. Below 1400px it wraps inside its own border rather than losing a part or clipping the bar.

Limits

Rules of composition and context, which no anti-rule can carry: nothing else is the right component to take, and what is wrong is the count or the neighbour. Counted on the grey corpus at stage 09, and every one of them is a function in design/kit/checks/rules.mjs. Full table with sources on Architecture.

  • R5. No shell before authentication, and no annunciator on a console that cannot read Clerk.

States

It has none. Read only in the MVP, and the strip reports rather than offers: no hover, no active, no focus ring, no out of reach. What looks like a state here is a reading, and a reading is decided by whether a tenant is selected rather than by anything the analyst does to the strip.

Narrow this page and watch the dividers go rather than the parts. Below 900px the strip takes a line of its own under the bar, wraps, and drops every |: a separator that wraps onto a line by itself is a character with no job. Nothing else is removed, because each of the three parts is a different claim and none of them is the summary of the other two.

Two declared departures, and both are written down rather than discovered. At 360 the type goes to 10.5px, one of the two places in the product below the 11px floor. And the | measures 1.18 against its ground and is meant to: WCAG 1.4.1 does not cover a purely decorative glyph, and the exemption is declared in design/kit/checks/contrast.mjs so a value that stops being exempt shows up as a new line instead of staying quiet. It is declared as ink so the surface rule can see it at all, which is the point of declaring surfaces.

One finding is open on it. The | has no aria-hidden, so a screen reader announces it three times per strip on every screen. That was raised at the step 4 gate as a finding rather than settled as a decision, and it is still a finding.

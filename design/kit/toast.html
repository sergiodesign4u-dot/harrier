Toast

A notice in Z6. 4 instances across 2 screens, and the whole zone was specified in prose and drawn nowhere until stage 07 step 9 found it.

Node 8.4 named three notices as MVP and not one of them existed. That is the cheapest kind of gap to find and the most expensive to leave, and it is why the stage contract is read as a checklist by an instrument that cannot see a step that never happened.

  • .toastthe notice. It stacks in Z6 and never covers the pane
  • .rolewho is speaking, in the argument note’s register at 9.5px. This is the machine reporting on itself rather than the product addressing the analyst

When to use it

In Z6, for something that happened as a result of what the analyst just did. It is not for the state of the screen: that is banner.

Where she meets it. Right after filing a verdict, which is the moment the product has to be certain. The alert form exists because the one thing worse than a failed write is a failed write she dismissed.

Rule and anti-rule

Do

ClerkThe write failed. The verdict is not recorded.

held until it is

It says what failed, what that means, and why it is still on the screen. No control, because there is nothing safe to do with it yet.

Do not

ClerkThe write failed.

Dismiss

Never a disabled dismiss. A control that is visibly there and cannot be used teaches nothing and reads as broken, and design principle 3 says out of reach is shown with the reason stated rather than hidden.

Limits

Rules of composition and context, which no anti-rule can carry: nothing else is the right component to take, and what is wrong is the count or the neighbour. Counted on the grey corpus at stage 09, and every one of them is a function in design/kit/checks/rules.mjs. Full table with sources on Architecture.

  • R3. No overlay covers the detail pane. Design principle 5: no overlay hides the evidence the analyst is deciding on.
  • R4. The notice layer shows at most three notices at the desk and at most one at 360, and a failure takes the single slot.

The toast itself has no states. Only the dismiss inside it does.

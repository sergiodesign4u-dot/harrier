01 · Two reasons, and the one thing it cannot inherit

The same case, two addresses neither redirects to the other

The pane. The list survives the decision, which is the guarantee 0.1 exists to make. Filing here leaves her on the next case
4.2 · /case/{id}
A permalink, and the only rendering at 360. The list is not here, so filing leaves her on a case she has already ruled on
Reason 1A permalink. The log, a ticket, a pager, a client email. An address that travels
Reason 2The only rendering at 360. 4.1 is the right pane of a split, and at 360 there is no split

4.2 is 4.1 without the list: the same ten blocks, same order, same bank rows. Repeating them here would create a second edition that drifts, so this node references rather than restates. What it cannot inherit is 0.1's guarantee, and that is not a defect. It is the cost of a permalink, and it produces the rule in section 02.

Latitude stays, and that is deliberate

It would be the easy thing to drop: a six row table on a 360 screen. It stays because at 03:00 the question "what has Clerk already done to this client, and what could it still do without me" is the fastest route to knowing whether this needs waking someone up. Compressed to the classes in force and the ones it had to ask about, without the reasons.

Escalate is pinned, and the tension with principle 5 is real

Principle 5 says no overlay hides the evidence the analyst is deciding on. A pinned bar covers a strip of text. We pin it anyway, and here is the argument rather than a shrug: the named scenario is read and escalate, and an action reachable only by scrolling past a full narrative is not the primary action of the surface. The bar is one control tall, the page scrolls under it, and she is not deciding here, she is handing over. Principle 5 protects a decision being made on evidence; this surface makes no decision.

What is lost, said plainly

A case she reads at 03:00 and knows to be benign cannot be closed from the phone. It stays in the queue until she is at a desk. That is the platform line applied honestly, and 4.4 records the same cost from the other side.

04 · Emotional support is reduced at 360, and that is coherent

At 4.1 the evidence block carries Emotional, P1, thorough rather than lucky by rendering what Clerk looked for and did not find with the same weight as what it found. At 360 that list is capped at three.

The mechanism is weakened, and it is the right trade, because the job at 360 is not to decide. The feeling of thorough against lucky attaches to a ruling, and no ruling is made here. What she needs instead is enough to justify waking someone up, which is a lower bar and an honest one.

Social, P1 is untouched: the verdict record is structured wherever it is written, and nothing is written here.

01 · Blocks, in priority order

Every one of them has a source or a job, and the sources were opened in session

Priority reasoned from the desk at 1440, which is the stance in CLAUDE.md, and then proved at 360. This is the first node where the bank does its work: the composition below is assembled from four references and matches none of them.

Deliberately absent, seven blocks all four references ship

  • Summary cards · saved views · column builder · density toggle · per column filter icons · chat composer
  • Bulk actions with checkboxes

Bulk actions are the load bearing absence. A verdict is a judgement on one case with its own evidence. Bulk accept is the fastest way to destroy the audit trail this product exists to produce, and it would make design principle 3 a lie. Each of the other six carries its reason in blocks.md.

02 · The row, and it is the canonical component

Defined once here; 5.1 reuses it, and anything that does not says why

Row anatomy seven slots, each earning its width
SeverityGlyph plus word
Redundant coding, so colour is not the only carrier. Taken from Defender
ClientTenant, same position always
Principle 4. The same signal is a Tuesday at one client and an incident at another
What it isThe shape of the case, short
Not a generated sentence. Defender's incident name truncates in the row, putting the least decidable content in the widest column
Clerk concludedThe draft verdict
The thing being ruled on. It is why the row exists at all
Cost to checkSize of the evidence, counted
Ours, no reference. The honest input to "what do I open next"
Stateescalated, unrecorded, Clerk acted
The visible states rule. A case that left her hands must not look like one that did not
AgeHow long it has waited
What is going cold

What the row must not carry: a checkbox, a bare priority score, or a truncating generated name. All three are named refusals from the bank rather than omissions.

06 · Addressing and permission

Private. noindex, no schema, no crawlable surface. This section is what replaces the SEO block a public product would carry.

Route/queue
With a case/queue/case/{caseId}. The list is part of the address, so the state is shareable and survives a reload
Scope in the URLFilters are addressable, so a scope can be handed to another analyst. Tenant is addressed by opaque id, never by client name, because a URL travels through pager, chat and ticket
HeadingOne h1, the queue and its scope. The list is a labelled region so assistive tech can name it without reading the shell
PermissionOnly tenants inside the analyst's provider scope. A scope naming a tenant outside it returns no results rather than an error, the same rule 8.1 carries: not found and not yours must be indistinguishable, or the URL enumerates other providers' clients
From a pagerLands here after 1.1 with the case selected, not on a bare queue. The block that makes this true belongs to 1.1

08 · Emotional support, and it is empty

The table in ia/docs/sitemap.md lists four emotional and social mechanisms. None of them lives on this node. They sit on C1 the case, A1 the brief, D1 the log and F1 the grants.

That is recorded rather than filled. Writing a mechanism here for completeness is exactly what the base layer's table was built to prevent.

One candidate, named for the table's owner rather than claimed here

What checking it will cost may support Emotional, P1, thorough rather than lucky, because it lets the analyst choose the order of work on evidence instead of on impulse. It is not in the table, so it is a question for stage 03a's table and not an entry in this node.

#Open questionWho settles it
1Does the row carry latitude, or only the annunciator? A per row marker for what Clerk was permitted to do at that tenant puts the differentiator on forty lines at once.

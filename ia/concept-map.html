Why the keyboard costs one more than the mouse, and why that is not a defect

A click names its target. A keystroke does not, so Enter stands between traversing the list and deciding on a case: arrows read, Enter decides. Without it, A pressed while scanning forty rows would file a verdict into an append only log that has no undo.

It is also what makes the single letter verdict keys legal at all. WCAG SC 2.1.4 allows a character key shortcut only if it can be turned off, remapped, or is active only on focus, and the pane holding focus is that third condition. The third keystroke is an interlock, not friction, and it is the cheapest of the three ways to satisfy the criterion.

The finding: rejecting costs twice what accepting costs

Design principle 3 says override is one key, and it teaches, and 4.4 argues in its own words that if rejecting Clerk costs more steps than accepting it, the analyst accepts. The tree now says accept is one keystroke after the interlock and reject is three.

Two of those three are the teaching, which is the half of the principle that makes rejection worth collecting at all, so the answer is not to delete them. 4.4 already spends its design on making them cheap: the pairing means five of six reasons need no second question, and there is no Next and no confirmation step. What stage 04 inherits is a measurement, not a slogan: draw the dialog so the reason list holds focus on open, and the third keystroke is the file. If it cannot, principle 3 is overstated and it is the principle that should be rewritten, not the count.

What flattening cost. The fleet has no menu item, so it is less discoverable. The secondary persona’s action sits at level 3 and is LATER, so in the MVP it is not reachable at all. The client summary is far from the case it grows out of.

Global, contextual, deep. Global: three items, plus 0.3 the tenant’s autonomy state in a fixed position, which is a requirement taken from the Flight Mode Annunciator rather than a preference, because a mode that has to be inferred is a display failure, plus 0.4 the connection strip. Contextual: 4.1 in the pane, 4.4 and 4.6 over it, 3.6 the scope bar. Deep: 7.2 changing a grant, 5.6 the log narrowed to one case.

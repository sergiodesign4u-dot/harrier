01 · The rule, and it is the whole node

Three different situations must produce one identical page.

Three causes, one rendering if any differs, the difference is the leak
CAUSE 1
Does not exist
the id is not a case anywhere
CAUSE 2
Not your provider
the case exists at a tenant your provider does not cover
CAUSE 3
Not your role
covered tenant, forbidden to you
Testable rather than aspirational

An outsider with a list of ids learns which are real; an insider learns which clients another provider holds. Stage 04 should test it literally: the three renderings must be byte identical, including the page title.

Grounded in the tenant data isolation requirement in CLAUDE.md. It is the same rule 1.1 applies to sign in copy and 3.6 applies to facet values. Three nodes, one rule, and this is where it is written down.

03 · What it must not say

NeverBecause
You do not have access to this caseConfirms the case exists. The leak, in one sentence
This case belongs to another tenantWorse: confirms it exists and that it is somebody's
Any client or tenant nameThe page can be reached unauthenticated
Check the link and try againBlames the sender when the cause may be scope, and implies the link was malformed, which is a false lead at 03:00

Stage 05 writes the words. This node fixes the constraints.

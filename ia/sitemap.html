Stage 03b · Information architecture, detail layer

Everything the product is made of

Eight screens expand into forty six nodes, because a dialog you have to design and a state you have to survive cost the same as a screen. This is the tree. What each node contains lives on its own page, reachable from Structure.

This is not the flow diagram. Flows answers whether a route reaches the goal and colours dead ends. This answers what exists and what contains what. The two disagree on purpose: a node that is a dead end in a flow is still a node that has to be designed.

8.3 was corrected at the stage 08 audit and is now LATER. This cluster used to route a tenant that is not this analyst's to 8.3. That is exactly what 8.1 forbids: if out of scope renders differently from not found, the address bar distinguishes "this client is somebody's" from "no such client". Out of scope now renders 8.1. What is left for 8.3 is a refusal by role, and the first one arrives with cluster 7. No node in this cluster is a dead end.

03 · What the expansion cost

Where the thirty seven extra nodes came from

SourceNodesWhat it means
The eight screens8Already on the concept map
States and dialogs inside them25Eight of these were already drawn in the flows at the base layer. They were routes then; they are nodes now
Discovered here, no parent screen12The whole shell, the session, the systemic cluster, and two canonical taxonomies found at the gate that closed the global elements. Scope set here rather than inherited. Eleven MVP; 8.3 became LATER at the stage 08 audit
The number that matters for the next stage

Thirty nine MVP nodes is the honest cost of this product, not the five screens the base layer counted. The estimate at step 6 decides how many get a black and white mockup and how many stay a written specification. That decision is deliberately not made here, because a map that trims itself to fit the budget stops being a map.

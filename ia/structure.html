02 · Block bank, by page type

Where the composition of a page comes from, so it is not taken from the air

Every other artefact in this pipeline has a rule of origin. The composition of a page did not: a template that says "blocks: 1, 2, 3" and does not say where they came from returns the median of everything the model has seen, and that median only becomes visible at stage 04, when the structure is already rolled out. So the source is fixed here, per page type rather than per node. Full bank in ia/docs/blocks.md.

A correction to what was said at the entry gate

At the gate I said no competitor publishes a page of this type outside a login, so the domain half of the bank would have to be our own research. That was wrong in a useful way: vendor documentation publishes screenshots of the real product, and those are public pages of exactly the right type. PagerDuty documents its Operations Console and Microsoft documents the Defender incident queue, both with the split pane visible. The domain half of this bank is real, not a substitute.

What the comparison column actually found

Three of our advantages are the same advantage: the references show numbers without their claim, their scope, their window or their count. 71%, 100, Thought for 20s. Each is legible and none is checkable. That is one differentiator, and calling it three would be inflating the count. The one structural difference is the panel: theirs previews and ours decides, and no bulk actions, no chat composer and verdict controls at the foot all follow from it.

Six blocks that all four references ship and we do not: summary cards, saved views, a column builder, a density toggle, bulk actions, a chat composer. Each with a reason in blocks.md. A composition that matched one source one to one would be a copy, and this one matches none.

What type B found, stated rather than implied

One block was worth the whole type: the greyed out action with its reason. Defender uses it for role based access; we use it for latitude. The analyst sees, on this case, what Clerk could have done here and could not, which is the differentiator doing work on the screen rather than on a settings page.

Basis 69 joins 100, 71% and Thought for 20s. Four references, four numbers, none naming its claim. Type A called this one differentiator and type B does not make it two; it makes it better evidenced.

Seven blocks the references ship in 4.1 and we do not: the incident graph, suggested prompts, Regenerate, the task pane, Open in Security Copilot, thumbs up and down, and the blanket AI disclaimer. The ten block composition for 4.1 is in blocks.md and matches no single source.

03 · How to read a chip

Three things carried by every node

CarriedWhy it is on the chip
NumberCluster and position. The number is the address used by every later stage, so a node renamed without renumbering breaks references rather than labels
TypePage, section, state, dialog, loading, empty, error. Twenty five of the forty five are not pages, which is the entire reason this layer exists
BorderSolid means the node has a written specification. Dashed means it is on the map and the work is declared. Grey background means LATER
What this hub is not

It is not the sidebar. The roadmap on the left tracks stages of the pipeline; this tracks nodes of the product. Forty five nodes in a sidebar would bury the twelve stages that still have to happen, so the two registries stay in separate namespaces and the sidebar never grows a node list.

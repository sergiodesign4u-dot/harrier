Where the composition of a page comes from, so it is not taken from the air

Every other artefact in this pipeline has a rule of origin. The composition of a page did not: a template that says "blocks: 1, 2, 3" and does not say where they came from returns the median of everything the model has seen, and that median only becomes visible at stage 04, when the structure is already rolled out. So the source is fixed here, per page type rather than per node. Full bank in ia/docs/blocks.md.

What the comparison column actually found

Three of our advantages are the same advantage: the references show numbers without their claim, their scope, their window or their count. 71%, 100, Thought for 20s. Each is legible and none is checkable. That is one differentiator, and calling it three would be inflating the count. Six blocks that all four references ship and we do not: summary cards, saved views, a column builder, a density toggle, bulk actions, a chat composer. Each with a reason in blocks.md. A composition that matched one source one to one would be a copy, and this one matches none.

03 · How to read a chip

Three things carried by every node

CarriedWhy it is on the chip
NumberCluster and position. The number is the address used by every later stage, so a node renamed without renumbering breaks references rather than labels
TypePage, section, state, dialog, loading, empty, error. Twenty five of the forty five are not pages, which is the entire reason this layer exists
BorderSolid means the node has a written specification. Dashed means it is on the map and the work is declared. Grey background means LATER
What this hub is not

It is not the sidebar. The roadmap on the left tracks stages of the pipeline; this tracks nodes of the product. Forty five nodes in a sidebar would bury the twelve stages that still have to happen, so the two registries stay in separate namespaces and the sidebar never grows a node list.

What the third instrument was worth

Three lines came back "was not done", and none of them was visible to the other two instruments. A deferred list that does not exist leaves no trace in any file; a cookie decision nobody made leaves no trace either; a stale count on a page from the previous stage is not a contradiction, because nothing else asserts the number. All three were omissions rather than errors, which is exactly the class a checklist finds and a reader does not.

What the two instruments actually proved

The overlap was one finding out of thirteen. Codex reads the source and found a map row never corrected upward and a name carried three ways. The browser reads the output and found a stylesheet collision and two real overflows. Neither could have found the other's list, and the biggest finding, three files routing an out of scope tenant to the wrong page, came from reading what the nodes require of each other rather than from either instrument's radius.

What the sweep now covers, and it is reusable

Every one of the 22 built pages, loaded in a 360 wide frame and measured: clientWidth 360, scrollWidth 360, zero overflowing. Every local link on every page fetched: zero broken. Em dash: zero. Backlink present on all 22. One pass, and it should run again at stage 04.

# wireframes/ is grey, and it stays grey

1. **No colour, type family, icons, illustration, shadows or motion in this folder.** Stages 06 to 08 arrive on **copies** under `design/`, never by painting `_wf.css`. Breaking this loses the stage's whole artefact, which is why the rule is also in the root `CLAUDE.md`.
2. **`_wf.css` is the one home of how a screen looks.** A token value never lives inline. A rule on two or more pages lives there. Anything inline is one use, written through `var()`, under a `/* INLINE: <screen> :: */` marker.
3. **Every state is its own page**, registered in `window.WF_NAV` in `_nav.js`, which also renders the panel, the coverage map and the state matrix. Register the state before drawing it.
4. **The generators in `docs/` are 61 pages BEHIND the corpus, and running one regresses its pages.** The rule used to read "pages come from the generators, a hand edit is lost the next time its generator runs", and that is exactly what happened: stage 05 edited the rendered output and never back-ported, so a generator still says `wants to contain` where voice ruled `proposes`, `Next` where microcopy ruled `Open your provider's sign in`, and it puts IA node numbers inside product copy where the register forbids them. Measured on 2026-08-26: re-running all thirteen changed **61 of 62 pages**, every one of them in visible text. **Do not run a generator to change a page.** Edit the page, mirror the change into its generator so the source does not carry the contradiction, and treat the two as out of step until somebody back-ports the stage 05 pass.
5. **Nothing is invented here.** A gap is fixed upward in `ia/docs/pages/`, then rendered. Interface strings are stage 05 drafts; author rationale is `.anote`, product copy is `.gnote`, and the two never share a register.
6. **Fixtures are canon.** `ia/docs/pages/reading-conventions.md` owns every name, id, count and timestamp. No new one without declaring it there.
7. **Accepted on the live URL at 1440, 1024 and a measured 360**, never on localhost and never on a resized window.
8. **The folder is no longer frozen, it is KEPT IN STEP with the coloured product.** The freeze held while the record was true. It stopped being worth keeping when the corpus went on asserting things the project had ruled false: a ruling for Aubrey Dental Group, the one client the canon gives none; Norsk Marine contained above a latitude of `Investigate`. Reconciled on 2026-08-26, and the state the freeze protected is the tag `wireframes-frozen-04`, which is what `census.md`, the stage 09 pattern counts and every pixel baseline are reproducible against. **What is carried back is a CONTRADICTION or a defect, never a stage boundary:** interface strings here stay stage 05 drafts by rule 5, and a screen that exists only in colour is not drawn here after the fact. The measure is `node design/kit/checks/diverge.mjs` plus a string diff of the paired pages; it stood at 41 of 62 pages differing and is now 0.
9. **A STRING DIFF AT ZERO DOES NOT MEAN THE TWO SIDES AGREE.** It proves they say the same words and says nothing about where the words sit: the pane foot held the same four buttons in the same order and drew them two by two here against Accept over three there, and no check in this project would have reported it. **Colour, type family, icons, shadows and motion are the stage boundary; where a block sits and how wide it runs is BLOCK PRIORITY, which is what this folder owns**, and it is carried back. The instrument is the arrangement diff: for every container of two or more children it reads how many rows they wrap into and each child's share of the container's content box, both scale free, so a different face and a different palette cannot register. It runs on 1217 shared containers over 61 paired pages and stands at 4 row divergences and 182 share divergences, all of them named in `docs/decisions.md` under 2026-08-26 and all of them type rather than structure. **Two of its own first readings were its own defects**, a baseline pixel counted as a row and a share taken of an inline child that has no box; a large number from this instrument is disbelieved before it is acted on.
10. **AND GEOMETRY CANNOT SEE A LEAF, WHICH IS WHY THERE ARE TWO INSTRUMENTS.** `14 signals` is a cell with no element children, so a container walk never reaches it: it broke onto two lines here and one in the product, and the arrangement diff called the page clean. `design/kit/checks/layoutprops.mjs` reads the DECISIONS instead, comparing on every shared element the computed properties that control layout and wrapping, with `max-width` and `min-width` compared as a KIND rather than a value because the pixel is where the font gets back in. **A difference in `white-space` is something a person wrote and a type face cannot produce one.** It opened at 2452 readings and 100 distinct over 5684 shared elements and stands at 54 and 7, every one of the seven named in `docs/decisions.md` with its reason for staying. **Run BOTH before believing the two sides agree**, plus the string diff and the gate at 1440 and 360, and remember that one of the carry-backs was itself a regression that only the next pass of the same instrument caught.
